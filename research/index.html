Research

The NK1R story, step by step

Scroll through a computational drug-discovery thesis: from target to leads to validation. Final-year thesis, University Institute of Medical Laboratory Technology, The University of Lahore (2025 to 2026).

1 / 5

STEP 01 — THE TARGET

A receptor for stress and anxiety

The human neurokinin-1 receptor (NK1R) is a GPCR target in stress and anxiety biology. The study aimed at its orthosteric pocket (PDB 6HLO), asking whether gut microbial postbiotic metabolites could modulate it, a gut-brain axis hypothesis.

STEP 02 — THE LIBRARY

52 metabolites, 4 approved drugs

52 gut-derived metabolites plus four approved NK1R antagonists (aprepitant, fosaprepitant, rolapitant, netupitant) were docked into the pocket with AutoDock Vina. The approved drugs served as the benchmark every candidate had to beat.

STEP 03 — THE LEADS

Urolithin B rises to the top

Urolithin B and a benzofuran-2-one derivative emerged as lead computational candidates: favourable docking scores, reference-like hydrophobic contacts, predicted blood-brain barrier permeability and low predicted toxicity (SwissADME, ProTox).

STEP 04 — THE VALIDATION

100 nanoseconds of proof

Density functional theory on the reference antagonists, then 100 ns molecular dynamics simulations of three antagonist-receptor complexes with MM-PBSA and MM-GBSA free-energy estimation. Docking poses that survive dynamics earn their keep.

STEP 05 — THE MANUSCRIPT

Written up for the world

Results prepared as a full research manuscript with structured figures and tables, formatted for journal submission. The toolkit: Vina, GROMACS, DFT, SwissADME, ProTox, pkCSM, STRING, Cytoscape.
