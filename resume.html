Research Experience

Final-Year Thesis Researcher, Computational Pharmacology

2025 to 2026 · University Institute of Medical Laboratory Technology, The University of Lahore

  • Designed and carried out an in silico study of gut microbial (postbiotic) metabolites as potential modulators of the human neurokinin-1 receptor, a target in stress and anxiety biology.
  • Docked 52 gut-derived metabolites and four approved NK1R antagonists (aprepitant, fosaprepitant, rolapitant, netupitant) into the orthosteric pocket of the receptor (PDB 6HLO) using AutoDock Vina.
  • Identified urolithin B and a benzofuran-2-one derivative as lead computational candidates with favourable docking scores, reference-like hydrophobic contacts, predicted blood-brain barrier permeability and low predicted toxicity.
  • Screened drug-likeness and toxicity with SwissADME and ProTox. Applied density functional theory to the reference antagonists and ran 100 ns molecular dynamics simulations of three antagonist-receptor complexes with MM-PBSA and MM-GBSA free-energy estimation.
  • Prepared the results as a full research manuscript with structured figures and tables, formatted for journal submission.
